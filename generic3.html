Logitech Z323 Speaker Covers

Type

Product Design

Software

Autodesk Fusion

Manufacturing

3D Printing

Genre

Design

I Have 2 Pairs of the Logitech Z323 and i've been wanting a more modern looking speaker set, so i decided to change that myself. For this project I designed a set of modern replacement covers from scratch and 3D printed them.

I modelled the covers in Autodesk Fusion. Every opening is positioned around the speaker's real layout, so the drivers, the bass port and the volume knob all stay uncovered and easy to reach.

The result is a cleaner, more modern desk setup, and the speakers stay fully usable with all their controls in reach.
